The Accounting team at Orion Technologies manages the company's financial reporting, budgeting, forecasting, and month-end close processes. Laura Martinez leads the team as Vice President of Finance and oversees a group of accountants, financial analysts, and accounting managers. The team works closely with the Accounts Payable and Accounts Receivable departments to ensure that vendor payments, customer invoices, and financial records are processed accurately and on time. Most of the Accounting team is based in Austin, while several employees work from the company's Seattle office.

In 2023, Orion Technologies appointed David Wilson as Director of Accounting to lead the company's financial reporting operations. David works closely with Laura Martinez on quarterly financial statements and coordinates with the Legal and Finance teams on compliance requirements. The Accounting team also supports Orion Cloud by tracking revenue, operating expenses, and infrastructure costs associated with the platform. As Orion Cloud expanded, the team created new financial reporting procedures to separate cloud-related expenses from the company's traditional software business.

In 2024, the Accounting team implemented a new financial management system to automate account reconciliation and improve the month-end close process. The project was sponsored by Laura Martinez and managed by David Wilson, with technical support from Orion Technologies' Enterprise Systems team. The new system reduced manual reconciliation work and gave senior management more timely access to financial reports. The Accounting team plans to extend the system to the Seattle office and integrate it with the company's procurement and payroll platforms.
